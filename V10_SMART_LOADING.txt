V10 — smart loading / no visual redesign

What changed:
- Loader minimum display is ~1.25s (hard cap ~3.2s); its PANXRT entrance is smoother.
- While the loader is visible, the browser prepares the first 10 WORK cards.
- First images are requested eagerly and decoded before/just after reveal.
- First few videos are allowed to preload in the background, but the loader never waits for full video downloads.
- As the visitor scrolls WORK, media roughly 1.5 screens ahead is prepared automatically.
- Videos farther down stay metadata/idle until they are approaching the viewport.
- Existing video playback cap / pause-offscreen behavior remains active.

Explicitly NOT changed:
- card sizes
- mosaic/grid layout
- image/video crop or focal point
- blur / frosted effects
- hover behavior
- section animations / timing
- captions
- colors
- typography
- case layout

This is a loading-strategy layer only.
