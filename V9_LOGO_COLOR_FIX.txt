V9 — progressive PANXRT color transition

Built from stable V8.

Changed only the PANXRT wordmark behavior:
- The logo is still one persistent positioned control, so it does not jump.
- It now contains two perfectly aligned visual layers: dark ink + paper.
- The paper layer is clipped by the exact same moving light/dark boundary used by the menu.
- As WORK crosses the wordmark, the letters recolor progressively at the physical boundary,
  matching the old visual behavior without switching between two separate logo positions.
- During section movement the wordmark position is driven by the same JS tween as the header,
  removing independent CSS lag.

All V8 stability fixes remain unchanged.
