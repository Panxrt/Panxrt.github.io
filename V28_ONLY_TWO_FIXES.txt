V28 — rebuilt directly from V26.

ONLY THESE TWO BEHAVIORS CHANGED:

1) Filter submenu tray
- .filter-reveal (the existing frosted 50px backing) now moves vertically.
- Filter buttons no longer have a separate vertical transform.
- Glass + filter controls therefore enter/leave as one physical tray.
- V26 height, left alignment, typography and glass recipe are preserved.

2) WORK -> CONTACTS clipping
- Removed the synthetic viewport*(1-contactProgress) cut.
- Header clipping now follows the actual physical LEFT edge of the WORK spine.
- The calculation also includes galleryProgress because the WORK spine slides
  one rail to the right while gallery mode is open.
- PANXRT and menu labels therefore share the same physical clipping boundary.
- No artificial delay was added to CONTACTS.

NOT TOUCHED:
- gallery/grid geometry
- card sizes/crop/hover
- WORK matte
- PANXRT movement itself
- page transition speed/easing
- bottom blur/gradient
- startup/loading
- cases/media
- filter size/alignment/glass look from V26
